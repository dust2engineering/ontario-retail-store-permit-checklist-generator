# Ontario Retail Store Permit Checklist Generator - Ontario

**City:** Vaughan | **Area:** 2000 sq ft | **Staff + typical customers (occupant guide):** 30

> Preliminary checklist generated with the free open-source Ontario Retail Store Permit Checklist Generator by Dust 2 Engineering Inc., Mississauga, Ontario. Verify every item with your designer and the local building department before submitting.

## 1. Application basics (always required)
- [ ] Municipal building permit application form, completed and signed by the owner or authorized agent
- [ ] Schedule 1: Designer Information form for each qualified designer on the project
- [ ] Owner authorization if the applicant is not the property owner, plus the lease for a tenant fit-out if the city asks for it
- [ ] Zoning review first: confirm the retail use, floor area and any outdoor elements are permitted at this address
- [ ] Occupant load calculation shown on the floor plan, based on the actual layout

## 2. Architectural drawings
- [ ] Floor plan: sales area, stockroom, office, fitting rooms and washrooms clearly labelled
- [ ] Barrier-free entrance, path of travel and barrier-free washroom where required
- [ ] Demising walls and fire separations between your unit and neighbours shown with ratings
- [ ] Exit signs, emergency lighting, travel distances and the number of exits for the sales floor
- [ ] Storage height and stockroom layout shown - high storage can change sprinkler requirements

## Storefront and facade
- [ ] Storefront drawings: new glazing, doors and any structural lintel changes
- [ ] Sign band and sign permit drawings - the sign permit is a separate application
- [ ] Landlord and plaza approvals for exterior changes, plus city facade rules in some downtown areas

## Change of use
- [ ] Change of use review: the existing unit must be checked against current code for retail use, even if construction is minor
- [ ] Washroom count, barrier-free and exit requirements are re-checked for the new use

## 3. Mechanical, plumbing and electrical
- [ ] HVAC drawings for the sales floor, stockroom and washrooms, including any rooftop unit changes
- [ ] Plumbing drawings for washrooms and any added sinks, with backflow preventer where required
- [ ] Electrical drawings: panel, sales-floor lighting, receptacles, exit signs and emergency lighting circuits
- [ ] Fire alarm verification if the building system is affected by the new layout; sprinkler requirements to be confirmed with the building department

## 4. Other approvals (not part of the building permit)
- [ ] Sign permit (separate), business licence from the city after the permit closes
- [ ] Check the lease and plaza rules for storefront, signage and construction hours before submitting

## Before you submit
- [ ] One combined PDF set: architectural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical and structural (if any), all consistent with each other
- [ ] All drawing sets use the same address, unit number, floor area and counts
- [ ] Designs that require an engineer must be stamped by a licensed Ontario P.Eng.; BCIN designer information where applicable
- [ ] Book a pre-consultation or zoning check with the city if the use or layout is not clearly permitted
